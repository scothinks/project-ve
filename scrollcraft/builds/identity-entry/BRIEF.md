## Three identity-defining ideas

1. Capability is something people build through practice.
2. Personal progress and collective progress belong on the same platform.
3. A clear structure makes new possibilities approachable.

Working positioning, authored for evaluation: **Build capability, for yourself
and your people.** This is a proposed promise, not an approved tagline. Learning,
practice and shared delivery are the substance; AI, XP, rewards and reporting
are supporting capabilities, not a checklist to draw into a logo.

Use **Project VE** in wordmarks and prose during the study. Keep VE uppercase.
Do not expand VE to “Values Education” in the new identity without a later
naming decision. No renaming is proposed. Organisation spelling follows the app.

## Eight creative decisions

All decisions below are authored under the delegation above, not user quotations.

| Topic | Direction |
|---|---|
| Vibe and references | Intelligent, open, capable, optimistic. References are spatial principles: a threshold opening into a room; a continuous folded strip; a modular construction; a tightly composed editorial masthead. |
| Journey | Understand the shared promise, recognise a personal or collective ambition, see how learning becomes practice, choose a route, enter with clear context. |
| Energy | A composed but distinctive opening, quiet explanation, one spatial transformation, then a settled decision. |
| Feeling and memory | Recognition → clarity → possibility → confidence → readiness. Remember one person's learning path expanding into a shared structure without losing that person's place. |
| Signature seed | “Your place in the whole”: switch between For myself and For my people; the same highlighted unit remains visible while its surrounding structure changes. This changes the example and destination emphasis without hiding either action. |
| Aesthetic range | Explore spatial editorial, energetic graphic, modular humanist and expressive typographic territories. Avoid a default dark luxury skin. |
| World or scenes | Distinct scenes in natural document flow. No continuous camera flight. No mandatory scroll before authentication. |
| Assets | No supplied brand kit for this exercise. Current colours, symbols and typography are explicitly excluded as inspiration. Editable vector studies are authored from scratch. Typography in prototypes uses installed system families; final font choice/licensing is a refinement task. No provider-generated imagery or paid calls. |

## Feeling curve, written before the scroll score

| Beat | Feeling | On-screen cause |
|---|---|---|
| Shared promise | Recognition | Both audiences are named, both actions immediately visible. |
| Learning into practice | Clarity | A short, quiet example connects a lesson to a real activity. |
| Your place in the whole | Possibility, the peak | One highlighted unit remains while the spatial composition grows into a shared learning structure. |
| Trusted context | Confidence | The platform and, where legitimately available, organisation identity sit together with a precise next destination. |
| Choose and enter | Readiness | The composition settles; both routes and sign-in stay readable and stable. |

Peak, in visitor language: “My learning path became part of something bigger,
and I could still see my place in it.” It lives in the third beat.
Tell-someone sentence: “It's the site where your own progress becomes a place
for your people to grow.” The sentence describes a proposed experience, not a
tested user reaction. Silence is quiet content before the peak, never an empty
viewport or a hidden call to action.

## Presentation and decision

`index.html` is a self-contained concept review board, with four territories,
actual-size icon studies, responsive welcome/authentication illustrations and
an adjustable spatial composition. It is not the final scroll site or live auth.
`ENTRY-PLAN.md` holds journey, screen-state and implementation acceptance plans.

Evaluate each territory on the same dimensions: personal invitation,
organisational credibility, small-size clarity, ownable visual character,
co-branding, broader system potential, and failure risk. Judgements are qualitative,
not user research or trademark clearance. Start by comparing **Aperture** with
**Assembly**. Select one territory before refinement; do not combine four marks.

## Competitive context, primary sources checked 7 September 2026

- [Sana](https://sanalabs.com/) frames its offer around AI for work and combines
  learning with enterprise capabilities. Implication: VE needs a human capability
  promise that can hold its own beyond an AI category label.
- [360Learning](https://360learning.com/) leads with an AI-driven LMS and a demo
  conversion. Implication: VE's two self-service routes should be explicit.
- [Brilliant](https://brilliant.org/) presents personal learning in math and
  coding. Implication: personal confidence and agency must remain visible even
  when VE speaks to institutions.

These are positioning observations from primary pages, not visual imitation,
market-share analysis or claims of feature parity. Detailed motion-reference
research belongs to the selected territory's production build.
