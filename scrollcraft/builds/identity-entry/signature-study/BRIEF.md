# Project VE signature study

7 September 2026. Signature-only exploration under explicit creative delegation.

## Fixed by the user

Aperture A.2, approved aubergine direction, Source Sans 3 / Source Serif 4
typography system, product grammar and tenant principles stay unchanged.
The canonical name is **Project VE**. Explore **project v/e** as its visual
signature, not a product rename. No production theme/application changes.

## Variables and decision

All signatures use Source Sans 3 semibold (600), matching the established
signature family/weight. Only case, word gap, tracking, slash treatment, optical
mark relationship and lockup arrangement vary.

- Current: Project VE; inherited tracking −0.025em, native word space.
- Tight: project v/e; −0.025em tracking, 0.18em word gap, slash 600 and native
  angle, no extra side spacing. Tests whether maximum cohesion is too compressed.
- Open (authored lead): project v/e; −0.018em tracking, 0.22em word gap,
  slash 500 at its native angle with 0.035em extra space per side. Keeps v/e
  together as a readable typographic sequence while reducing slash emphasis.
- Steeper: Open's spacing, slash 400 rotated −6° relative to the native glyph.
  This is a limit test, not a recommendation. A specially angled, thin stroke
  adds an unnecessary custom gesture and weakens small-size consistency.

The slash is the font's U+002F glyph throughout. It is not drawn as SVG, extended
beyond the type, separately coloured, extracted as an icon or made into a second
brand device. No font-family redesign, new typeface, palette or shape proposal.

The lead keeps the mark at the established 24px height in product signatures,
with a 7px gap. Large specimens use the same 0.95em mark height as before.
The lowercase silhouette looks quieter than the current capitals; increasing
the mark to compensate would let it dominate. Both are centred optically within
the same lockup footprint. Small signature minimum proposed at 16px text and
24px mark; 14px is shown as a stress test, not recommended production use.

Compact signature keeps the same-sized words on two lines: project, then v/e,
with a single A.2 alongside the combined word block. No oversized v/e or isolated
slash. Use only where horizontal width is constrained and vertical space is
available; do not squeeze this taller lockup into the existing product header
or tenant endorsement. Horizontal is the lead for those contexts.

## Comparison protocol

1. View current and three typographic treatments in the same neutral specimen.
2. Inspect lead/current at 14, 16, 20, 24 and 32px and in compact form.
3. Compare current and lead directly in exact copies of existing learner,
   organisation and tenant markup, with existing CSS and palette values.
4. Switch light/dark and tenant identity; compare both signatures under the
   same conditions. At phone widths the same pair stacks, preserving the actual
   mobile surface composition and a clear Current/Proposed label.

The context builder reads existing source rather than redrawing product UI.
Only signature contents change inside the comparison. The learning item, Current
state, programme/cohort content, layout rules and tenant endorsement hierarchy
are carried forward unchanged. Mark path/font file hashes and context markup
are checked against the preceding study. Source remains isolated.

## Recommendation to review

Open is the working lead because the slash reads as punctuation within v/e and
the lowercase rhythm is calm. The current Project VE signature is shorter and
more immediately identical to the canonical name. The proposed spelling could
suggest “v slash e” or a web path when read aloud; that is an audience-validation
question, not an established result. Accessible names, titles, ordinary product
copy and references continue to use Project VE. No final signature adoption is
assumed.
