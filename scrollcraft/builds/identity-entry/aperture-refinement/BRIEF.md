# Aperture refinement: the premise has won, not the mark

7 September 2026. Refinement follows the user's selection of the Aperture
territory, not approval of its Round 1 symbol, typography or palette.

## User direction

> I wouldn't select Aperture as final yet.
> I'd select Aperture as the territory to refine ... the premise has won, not the mark.

Three explicit demands govern this round:

1. Explore several ownable aperture geometries whose negative space relates to
   VE, progression or “your place in the whole”, without a VE monogram puzzle.
2. Reduce architectural literalness. Test symbols alone, without colour or wordmark.
3. Prove one underlying grammar from favicon to Project VE mark, learner surface,
   organisation surface and organisation co-branding.

The audiences remain equal. The intended entry actions remain Start learning and
Create an organisation. Creative direction is delegated. The prior concept study
is preserved; final assets and product implementation remain unapproved.

## What is being tested

Aperture means **an intentional opening in a supportive structure**. It need not
be an arch, doorway, window, camera iris or building. The shape should communicate
space and possibility before a literal object.

Four hypotheses are drawn on a 96-unit field and judged first in solid black:

| Study | Geometric idea | Negative-space relationship | Main competing reading |
|---|---|---|---|
| A: Offset | Asymmetric lozenge with one rising open edge and an offset inner space | The opening continues beyond its containing structure; a protected place remains | Chevron, bracket or interlocking link |
| B: Counterform | Two unequal opposing cuts around a diagonal interval | Two sides support one shared space; progression is the interval, not an arrow | Transfer symbol or folded ribbon |
| C: Orbit | Broad circular enclosure interrupted toward the upper right | A continuous support surrounds a nonconcentric core with an exit | Camera, refresh, loading or generic C |
| D: Release | A sloped enclosure opens at its upper edge | The inward notch narrows into a V-like relationship without drawing V and E | Shield, badge or downward motion |

Study A is the lead **hypothesis** because its opening, inset and offset can govern
real layouts. Study B is the counterproposal because it reduces the architectural
reading further. C and D stress-test whether softer enclosure or a V-adjacent
negative space improve the premise; current judgment is that their familiar
camera/shield readings weaken distinctiveness. No trademark clearance is implied.

## Naked-symbol protocol

The board opens with four unlabelled black marks (A-D) and their actual 16px forms.
The rationale is hidden until requested. Use the first association and whether the
silhouette remains distinguishable at 16/24/32px before evaluating explanation.
Switch to white-on-black and compare the original upright aperture as a reference.

The designer's inspection is a design critique, not external audience research.
The board provides a local observation field and records no submissions. The
user can conduct an unprompted first-association review with others before the
rationale is revealed. Suggested recognition criteria: no building as the dominant
association, a stable silhouette at 16px, and distinguishability from the other
studies. These are decision criteria, not measured results.

## One grammar, increasing scope

The lead grammar has three relationships, not five unrelated logo placements:

1. **Supported core:** generous clear space protects the relevant person/item.
2. **Open edge:** one controlled release indicates continuity, never a missing UI border.
3. **Offset focus:** the currently relevant unit is intentionally displaced from
   perfect symmetry, creating direction without an arrow or literal letter.

| Scale | What stays | What changes | Functional evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Favicon | Outer silhouette and uninterrupted negative channel | Dedicated pixel-grid geometry at 16/24px; full geometry at 32px | CSS displays actual sizes; no colour distinction required |
| Platform mark | Same protected void and opening | Full geometry plus neutral Project VE wordmark and clear space | Wordmark does not invent a new symbol |
| Learner surface | Core/edge/focus relationships | Opening becomes a corner treatment on the selected learning region, not a mask over text | Focus switch highlights one real sample item and preserves reading order |
| Organisation surface | Same learning item and attention grammar | Supporting structure expands into a programme/cohort context | Shared focus control updates the same item in both surfaces; item identity survives scope change |
| Co-branding | Platform signature and clear-space rules | Organisation's own monogram, type, colour and support details remain its own | Sample org switch changes only org slot/accent; platform mark and layout rules remain fixed |

The shared opening/corner angle is a family resemblance, not a requirement to
cut every rectangle. Forms, tables and accessibility outlines retain normal
rectangular bounds. Grouping and hierarchy carry the idea where decorative shape
would impair use. No counters, XP balances, testimonials or live metrics are invented.

## Optical sizing and colour

Dedicated tiny variants use fewer fractional coordinates and a wider clear
channel. They are labelled optical studies, not production-ready exports.
The same negative-space relation remains at every size; no tiny letter puzzle is
introduced. A black/white system toggle proves attention with weight, a border
and an explicit Current label, independent of the provisional blue/citron palette.

The first board's colour is retained only as a controlled variable for system
evaluation. It is not approved or a requirement. Typeface in this round is a
neutral system sans so custom typography cannot rescue weak geometry.

## Scroll-craft relationship

This is an identity grammar study preceding the connected #91 experience.
It advances the already authored “Your place in the whole” idea: expand the
supporting structure while the user's current place remains visible. The product
surfaces are explicitly labelled sample layouts. No full scroll delivery, imagery,
video, performance budget or rendering technology is being approved here.

For the eventual welcome: context widens around a stable focus; independently
moving support planes give depth while text/actions remain still. Learner and org
entry remain available without the motion. The original entry plan's auth, return
path, tenant, fallback and performance constraints remain authoritative.

## Next review

Choose a geometry hypothesis to continue, or reject all four if the naked symbol
still relies on explanation. Then refine ratios, optical sizes and typography;
validate first associations with people unfamiliar with the premise. Neither this
board nor a preferred candidate is a final identity asset or production release.

## Visual iteration

The first Offset draft resembled an angular C in its naked screenshot. It was
replaced with an asymmetric lozenge enclosure and dedicated optical variants.
This removes the regular C silhouette but still needs testing against familiar
chevron/link associations. Counterform also has a finance/transfer-symbol risk;
its attractive negative space alone does not establish distinctiveness.
