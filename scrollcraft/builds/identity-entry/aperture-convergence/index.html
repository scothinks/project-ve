Terminal angles. Upper cut changes from about 41° to 61°; lower cut from 39° to 50°. The two ends no longer repeat almost the same direction.

Inner space. Unequal sides and eased turns replace the regular sharp diamond. The lower arm stays longer and more supportive.

Remaining risk. A broken link or rounded bracket may still be seen. These are design hypotheses, not tested recognition or clearance claims.

Openness with a particular shape.

The upper arm shelters an offset core. The longer lower arm carries it. The release remains visible without completing a letter or a second link.

96-unit field. No interlacing, arrowhead or upright architectural axis. The faint construction lines are guides, never part of the mark.
