# Aperture Study A: convergence

7 September 2026. Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.

## Governing decision

The user selected Aperture Study A only for convergence. The mark is not final.
Preserve openness, support, asymmetry, scalability and non-architectural character.
Make negative space intentional; reduce link, chevron and generic technology
associations. No hidden letters or VE legibility requirement. Explore the identity
around a sufficiently stable geometry. Old blue and typography are provisional.
Learners and organisations retain equal priority and direct entry actions.
Production theme, auth and application source are outside this iteration.

## Authored direction

- Vibe: assured, generous, human, quietly distinctive. No new reference brand.
- Journey: naked geometry → signature → colour → the same place at wider scopes
  → small-size and token evidence. A design review, not a marketing scroll site.
- Energy: quiet scrutiny, expressive identity, calm usable product.
- Feeling curve: recognise A; understand the opening; feel human warmth; see
  continuity in organisation context; trust the restraint in tenant context.
- Peak: “My place stays visible as the structure around it grows.” The paired
  learner/organisation sample demonstrates this via one shared Current control.
- Tell-someone sentence: “It's the study where the same learning item keeps its
  place as the organisation comes into view.”
- Range/grammar: editorial specimen followed by a working surface. Natural
  reading, no pinning or camera flight. Deliberately static geometry inspection.
- Assets: native SVG lineage from A; locally bundled Source Sans 3 and Source
  Serif 4 variable fonts from google/fonts with original OFL notices. No imagery.

The established scroll-craft journey is carried forward as identity grammar;
this board does not claim a completed cinematic welcome, shipped site, or media
performance validation. Previous ENTRY-PLAN.md remains applicable to future #91.

## Geometry decision for this study

Compare inherited A with two close refinements, A.1 and A.2, at the same black
size. A.1 retains crisp corners but changes the two terminal slopes. A.2 carries
that unequal opening into a gently eased inner basin and blunted outer turns.
A.2 is the authored working master throughout the identity, not a final choice
on the user's behalf. Identity controls never change the geometry.

Unlike inherited A's repeated diagonal diamond, A.2's upper terminal is steep,
its lower terminal shallow, and its inner basin has unequal sides. The shorter
upper arm shelters; the longer lower arm supports. No interlacing, duplicated
links, sharp arrowhead, roof/base axis or letter construction is introduced.
It may still read as a broken link or rounded bracket: designer inspection is
not evidence that associations or trademark conflicts have been eliminated.

Full coordinates are in geometry.js, on a 96-unit field. Dedicated 16/24px
optical drawings widen the throat and simplify the inner easing. 32px and above
use the master. Clear space starts at one quarter of the 96-unit field. These
are review rules, pending print/raster and external recognition tests.

## Identity hypothesis

Primary: carbon ink, warm paper and deep aubergine, with a pale mineral focus
surface. Soft apricot is an editorial supporting field, never the sole status
signal. An alternate deep teal palette is a controlled colour comparison on
the identical mark, type and layouts. No coloured mark is needed in tenant UI.

Recommended signature: Source Sans 3 semibold, Project VE on one baseline,
equal weight, restrained negative tracking. Its humanist detail counters the
geometry without inventing bespoke letter tricks. Compare Source Serif 4 in the
signature before deciding: its editorial voice may feel more institutional.
Source Serif 4 is recommended only for selected expressive headlines; Source
Sans 3 carries navigation, labels, body and product density. Neither is final.

Fonts: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sourcesans3 and
https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/sourceserif4. Original font files
and OFL notices are bundled; no production font loading decision is implied.

## Grammar and tokens

Supported core = text retains readable rectangular bounds and clear spacing.
Open edge = a restrained released upper-right background corner on the current
learning item; never clip its text, controls, border focus ring or forms.
Offset focus = one explicit Current label, heavier title and leading rail.
Item name, order and state stay identical as programme and cohort context grow.

Tenant name, colour, type and monogram remain tenant-owned. Platform endorsement
is smaller and neutral, separated by clear space. Three fictional tenant
identities deliberately stress warm, cool and nearly-black palettes. Tenant
colour is confined to the identity slot; it cannot recolour status or platform
actions. The sample selector is not a tenant theme API.

identity-tokens.json is a standalone draft, with semantic light/dark roles,
space, type, focus and shape values. It is not imported by the Project VE app.
Contrast checks cover the declared text/action/focus pairs, not every potential
future tenant colour or a full accessibility audit. Colour is never a status
substitute. Success/error/warning semantics remain future product work.

## Review boundary

Inspect A.2 naked first. Then assess whether signature and colour make the same
geometry more convincing across scales. Do not reopen B/C/D or other territories.
Remaining judgement: whether the less regular counterspace is distinctive enough
without explanation; whether aubergine/Source Sans is the right human tone.
No production adoption, final vector master, legal clearance, or external
recognition result is asserted.
